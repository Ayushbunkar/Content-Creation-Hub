How did the world's most powerful AI company face a classified counterintelligence nightmare? 

Behind Nvidia's trillion-dollar Blackwell superchips lies an intelligence battleground. 

Foreign agencies realized they couldn't breach multi-layered cyber firewalls. So they targeted the human element. 

Classified inquiries probed covert associations, unauthorized data corridors, and executive vulnerability. 

When strategic dominance over global AI hangs in the balance, personal connections become the ultimate backdoor. 

Corporate boards launched emergency audits, foreign travel was scrutinized, and internal governance tightened overnight. 

Because in the silicon cold war, the most dangerous weapon isn't malware. It's human compromise.
